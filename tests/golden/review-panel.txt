- note: first, one to three sentences of reasoning: what the source shows, and what is right or wrong.
- fields: judge each of these parts as ok, wrong (or missing when needed) or unsure:

  attribute: Attribute (The property, stated specifically enough)
  value: Value (Digits, bounds and ranges)
  unit: Unit (Including scale factors; wrong if missing)
  conditions: Conditions (Load case, scenario, scope; wrong if a needed one is missing)
  basis: Basis (Requirement, target, measured, calculated... 'unknown' is right only when the source doesn't say; it is wrong for a 'shall' or 'must' (required) or a reported measurement)
  approximate: Approximate (Readings off charts should be approximate)
  quote: Quote (Verbatim, and supports the claim)
  section: Section (The section it was attributed to)
- verdict: exactly one of
  correct: Correct (A faithful reading of the source (usefulness is judged by flags))
  flawed: Usable but flawed (The core fact is right, but a qualifier, quote, scope or form is off, or part of the truth is stated as the whole)
  wrong: Wrong (The fact is misread, misbound or not supported)

- flags: every problem that applies (none if it is correct), from
  entity_misbound: Value bound to the wrong component or thing (e.g. Pump B's 45 kW recorded as Pump A's)
  scope_level_error: Wrong level: per-unit vs total, component vs system (e.g. a 2 kW per-module rating claimed as the whole array's power)
  attribute_misassigned: Right thing, wrong or overstated property (e.g. peak power recorded as continuous rating; 'composition: glass' when glass is one of several materials)
  relation_direction_reversed: Directed relationship reversed (e.g. 'HX-1 feeds P-2' when P-2 feeds HX-1)
  referent_ambiguous: Entity too vague to identify (e.g. entity 'the unit' or 'system' with no way to tell which)
  aggregation_unsupported: Computed, summed or inferred value presented as stated (e.g. 'total 120 MW' when the source only lists 3 x 40 MW)
  value_misread: Wrong digits, sign or decimal point (e.g. 0.35 read as 3.5)
  unit_wrong_or_missing: Unit wrong, missing or mis-converted (e.g. psi recorded as kPa)
  scale_factor_missed: A multiplier in a header or caption ignored (e.g. a 'Cost ($k)' column recorded as dollars; % vs fraction)
  bound_or_range_collapsed: A limit, range or tolerance reduced to one number (e.g. '<= 85 dBA' recorded as 85 dBA; '10 +/- 0.5' as 10)
  approximation_misflagged: Approximate or estimated reading marked exact (or vice versa) (e.g. '~12 t', or a value read off a chart, marked exact)
  value_fabricated: Value not in the source at all (e.g. a typical steel density filled in)
  condition_dropped: An operating condition or scope left out (e.g. efficiency without 'at 50% load')
  condition_misattached: A condition belonging to something else (e.g. the hot-day condition attached to the ISO rating)
  basis_wrong: Requirement, target, estimate and measurement confused (e.g. a 'shall be >= 95%' requirement recorded as a measured result)
  time_or_version_wrong: Wrong year, phase, revision or life stage (e.g. end-of-life capacity reported as beginning-of-life)
  scenario_confused: Baseline, option or case values swapped (e.g. option B's mass attributed to the baseline)
  polarity_or_negation_error: Negation or limit direction lost (e.g. 'shall not exceed 60 C' recorded as 'is 60 C')
  quote_not_verbatim: Quote paraphrased or stitched together (e.g. a quote joining two cells)
  quote_not_supporting: Quote exists but doesn't support this value or entity (e.g. the quote names Pump A; the value is Pump B's)
  region_wrong: The highlighted region doesn't contain the claim
  extrinsic_knowledge: From general knowledge, not this document (e.g. a standard material property inserted)
  confidence_miscalibrated: Model confidence clearly too high or too low (e.g. 0.95 on a value misread from a log axis)
  table_header_misaligned: Wrong row or column header (incl. multi-level) (e.g. value taken from the 'Max' column instead of 'Nominal')
  table_note_lost: Merged cell, footnote or note qualifier lost (e.g. footnote 'a) at 20 C' ignored)
  chart_axis_misread: Axis misread: interpolation, log scale, second axis (e.g. 300 on a log axis read as 30)
  chart_series_swapped: Legend or series mixed up (e.g. the design A curve read as design B)
  chart_trend_wrong: Direction or size of a change wrong (e.g. 'rises sharply' for a flat line)
  diagram_edge_invented: Connection that isn't in the diagram (e.g. a valve linked to a nearby but unconnected tank)
  diagram_edge_direction_reversed: Arrow or flow direction reversed
  drawing_callout_misbound: Dimension, tolerance or note on the wrong feature (e.g. +/-0.05 applied to the wrong bore)
  not_atomic: Several facts fused into one claim (e.g. 'mass 12 t and height 90 m' as one value)
  trivial_or_boilerplate: True but useless for comparison (e.g. a page number, drawing scale or revision date)
  kind_wrong: Claim kind wrong (text, table, chart, diagram)
  nearby_claims_missed: Clearly important facts next to it were not extracted (a coverage signal: say what was missed in the note)
- clarity: one of
  clear: Clear (I could judge this item)
  context_insufficient: Context insufficient (I'd need more of the document than shown to judge)
  source_illegible: Source illegible (The image or text can't be read well enough)
  reading_unclear: Not sure how to read the source (e.g. a chart or drawing convention I don't know; a domain expert might)
  item_ambiguous: Item or question ambiguous (The item could be read several ways, or the categories don't fit it)
- confidence: your confidence in your own verdict, one of
  high: High (I'd stake a review comment on it)
  medium: Medium (Probably right)
  low: Low (A guess, or outside what I know)
