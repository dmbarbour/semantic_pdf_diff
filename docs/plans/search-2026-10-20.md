# Search beyond the words typed

- **Status:** Active (2026-10-20)
- **The owner (2026-10-20):** "Broader search is good." (built as the [portable reports](portable-reports-2026-10-19.md)' milestone 6: words, phrases, fields, the most relevant first, `like:ID`). Then, on the four ideas proposed beside it: "I like all the ideas for search. So, lowest hanging fruit first, I guess."
- **Earlier (2026-10-20), on unrelated pairs:** "As a user of the report, it would be convenient to have an easily searchable database of claims even without the comparisons."

## Why

The page's search finds the words typed, wherever a claim holds them. It misses three kinds of match:
- **Another name:** two documents name one thing differently ("rated output", "nominal power"; 8 of the turbines run's 32 related pairs share words in both entity and attribute).
- **Another unit:** "75 kW" and "0.075 MW".
- **Another wording of the meaning.**

It also only searches inside one report; a store's claims can't be searched without writing one.

## Milestones

In order of effort, lowest first.

1. **Counterparts** (done 2026-10-20): the names the documents differ on, from the comparison's own pairs.
   - **In the claims view, with a search:** the claims the findings pair with the found ones are shown too, after them, each marked as a counterpart; a checkbox leaves them out.
   - **The other source's words:** where a found claim's entity or attribute holds the words searched and its counterpart's doesn't (the query doesn't find it), the counterpart's name is offered as a search: "rated output" offers "nominal power".
   - Nothing is added to the file: findings already pair the claims.
   - **On the turbines proposals run** (NREL 5 MW against IEA 15 MW, 711 claims, 128 related findings; 111 pair attributes worded differently):
     - "height above ground" finds 2 claims and 4 counterparts, and offers "height", "hub height" and "ground clearance".
     - "hub height" offers "height above ground" and "overall height", and "tower" for the found claim's entity "hub".
     - "rated speed" offers "Rotor Speed".
2. **Search from the command line** (done 2026-10-20): `semantic-pdf-diff search WORDS --store DIR [--store DIR ...]`.
   - It searches every claim of the stores, with or without a report. It's the database of claims the owner asked for, kept where the stores are.
   - The same query language, fields and ranking as the page: a Python port of `search.js`, held to the page by the same test cases, run by both.
   - It prints claims, most relevant first, with where each is; `--json` for tools; counterparts from the stores' comparisons.
   - **Built:**
     - Each store's latest comparison is searched (`--comparison N` picks another), several stores in one index.
     - The cards are written by `report.Cards` and read by `search.py` exactly as the page reads them.
     - Options: `--findings`, `--side` (a source's name or a part of it), `--alone` (no counterparts), `--limit`, `--json`.
     - A word to leave out needs no quoting (`search pump -gearbox`): the command has no `-h`.
   - **The same answers as the page:** every test case run in headless Chromium is run by `search.py` too.
     - On the turbines and TS 38.300 reports, 17 queries returned the same claims in the same order and offered the same names, up to 2,919 claims a query.
     - TS 38.300's 13,309 claims are indexed in 2.4 s; a search then takes 0.03–0.17 s.
   - **Found on the way:** a bare word searched the file's name too, so "mw" found all 711 of the turbines' claims (`nrel-5mw-p25-36.pdf`). Both now search a file's name only for `file:`.
3. **Numbers across units** (done 2026-10-20): `>50kW`, `>50 kW`, `value:10-20mm`, `<=0.1MW`, `value:75kW`.
   - **A claim's value** (`values.value_range`) is a number, a range (50–60, 1.2 to 3) or a bound (≤ 39, > 5), with its unit, or one written in the value ("75 kW"). In the page it's [kind, the unit's size, low, high]: 594 of TS 38.300's 13,309 claims and 579 of the turbines' 711 have one.
   - **Units** (`values.SEARCH_UNITS`) come from three tables:
     - the product's SI table;
     - the search's own (`SEARCH_EXTRA`: N, kN·m, N·m², kg/m, kg·m², tonnes, A, V, rad, rad/s and more);
     - the scorer's, put into SI through a unit both hold (its inches become metres through "m").
     The product's and the scorer's are unchanged: the product's enters comparison prompts.
   - **Spellings** are read alike in both engines: "N•m2", "N*m^2" and "N.m^2" are one unit; "kN-m" is "kN.m"; "º" is a degree; a unit lowercased where that's unambiguous ("RPM"; not "mw", which is mW or MW).
     - Across 42 local reports, 10,580 claim units are known and 1,272 aren't. The unknown are mostly not units: "-", "?", "% span", feet and inches already split.
   - **Matching:**
     - A comparison is a filter: its claims rank by the query's words.
     - A claim's range or bound is found where it meets the query's.
     - Without a unit, numbers compare as written.
     - A unit the search doesn't know finds nothing and is named beside the count.
   - **The page carries** the claims' values and the units table: TS 38.300's page grew 16 KB (0.9%), the turbines' 18 KB.
   - **On the turbines:** "height >100 m" finds 4 claims (150 m hub heights); "power >10MW" 11; "value:5-10MW" 7. On every query the page and `search.py` gave the same claims in the same order.
4. **Names from the model** (a lever, measured): synonyms for the claims' names, asked in or beside the topics' request.
   - **Built** (2026-10-20) as `search_names`, off until measured:
     - After the comparison, the model is shown every claim's item and property names, each source's, with how many claims use each. It groups those meaning the same (`synonyms.py`).
     - The names go in one request, or a request a topic past 400 names (the most even way's topics).
     - Answers keep only names as listed; a failed request leaves its names ungrouped.
     - Groups aren't joined where they share a name: joined, a large report's requests chained "tower" to "tower top" and "tower base moment".
   - **In the search** (page and command line): a query that names one of a group's names (every word of it and no more) finds the others' claims and findings too.
     - Another name is a claim's whole item or property, as written, not a phrase inside one: matched as phrases, "diameter" for "rotor diameter" added 47 claims.
     - They come after the rest at half their score, marked "found by another name", and are offered beside the other source's names. The counterparts checkbox (and `--alone`) leaves them out.
   - **Measured** (2026-10-20, $0.008 for three runs' requests, recorded) on recorded proposals runs. The test: the judged equivalent and different pairs whose properties are worded apart; a search for one claim's property, as typed, finds the other claim?

     | Run | Claims | Searches | Requests | By words | With the model's names | Added a search | Of those, judged related |
     |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
     | IEA report against its workbook | 3,133 | 212 | 16 | 53% | 66% | 2.3 | 18% |
     | Turbines (v5) | 1,409 | 122 | 16 | 55% | 67% | 2.0 | 34% |
     | Drawings | 554 | 112 | 1 | 75% | 75% | 0.0 | — |

     - "Judged related" counts the added claims the judge paired with a claim of the name searched. It's a floor: the judge saw only retrieval's candidates, under a cap.
     - By eye, most added claims are the same property under another name ("integral gain Ki" adds the "Integral Gain" claims; "rated rotor speed", "rated speed"; "base density", "effective density" and "mass density"). A few are another item's: "IEA Wind Task 37 reference turbine" was grouped with the 15-MW turbine.
     - Before the prompt ruled out parts, more general names and items numbered apart, and before whole names: 77% and 70% found, but 4.6 and 4.8 claims added a search, 15% and 17% judged related.
     - The drawings gain nothing: their misses are wordings within one source ("revision mark 4 date" against "revision 4 date").
   - **A default or not** is the owner's call. It costs about $0.004 a comparison of 1,400 to 3,100 claims, and changes only what a search finds.
   - Measured by how many related pairs a search for one name finds both of, against counterparts alone.
   - Pennies a comparison.
5. **By meaning** (a lever, measured): claims embedded by a model (DeepInfra serves Qwen3-Embedding).
   - The command line embeds the query too, so it can search by meaning.
   - The page can't embed a query offline, but `like:ID` by meaning works from the claims' vectors, if they fit the budget: 13,000 claims at 256 dimensions, a byte each, are 3.4 MB.
   - Measured on the related pairs words miss.
