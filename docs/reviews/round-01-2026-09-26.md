# Round 1: context levers and a table filter

- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Plan:** [query improvement in rounds](../plans/query-improvement-2026-09-26.md)
- **Research:** [round-01 context heuristics](../research/round-01-context-heuristics-2026-09-26.md)
- **Round folder:** `benchmarks/rounds/r01/` (round.json, variants, pairwise batches, decisions)
- **Baseline:** prompt v6, the recording of 2026-09-26 on all twelve slices (4,188 answers, $0.83). Round 1 is judged on the development set, extraction only.
- **Judges:** google/gemini-3.1-pro, Qwen/Qwen3.5-397B-A17B, XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Pro (reliability per dollar in s01 and s02). Each unit is judged in both orders.

## Predictions (written before the results)

| Variant | Settings | Prediction | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **table-filter** | `table_filter: true`, compared page by page | Wins on drawing sheets and chart pages (it removes nonsense rows from false tables); neutral on reports; fewer requests | Losing a real fact found only by a false "table" row |
| **neighbours** | 400 characters before and after each text chunk | Modest win on text units: fewer vague entities ("scoring"), better conditions | Claims taken from the context, or duplicated from neighbours |
| **text-layer** | 1,500 characters of a region's text layer with each image | Win on visual units, largest on drawing sheets (small labels spelled right) | Longer prompts; the model copying text-layer fragments as claims |
| **lead-in** | 400 characters of text above a table with each row | Small win on table units (rows gain their table's subject) | Few real tables in the development set, so a weak signal |

A variant is accepted when its win rate's 90% interval lies above 0.5 and no kind of input loses clearly (interval entirely below 0.45).

## Results: confounded, paused at $10.03

**Round 1 measured mostly run-to-run variation, not the levers.**
- Every variant re-recorded every request: the recording fingerprint included all the lever settings, even for requests a lever doesn't change.
- gemma-4 on DeepInfra isn't deterministic at temperature 0, so each variant's tile and overview answers were fresh samples, not the baseline's.
- The judges' notes confirm it. On a drawing sheet the "variant" read the finished floor as 2'-5½" where the baseline read 2'-9½", which has nothing to do with the table filter. On a turbine page one run kept a pitch angle's minus sign and the other dropped it.

**What was measured:**

| Variant | Units | Win rate (90% interval) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| table-filter (page units) | 48 | 0.43 (0.35–0.51) | complete; inconclusive |
| neighbours, text-layer, lead-in | 8 each, partly | — | judging paused |

- **By document** (table filter): rules 0.19 (3 pages), NREL 0.32 (12), ken-cd 0.34 (4), dc-cd 0.40 (4), IEA 0.48 (12), dc-manual 0.64 (7), LCIT 0.61 (3). These sample sizes are too small to read into.
- **It's a useful noise measurement,** close to an A/A test for everything but tables. Two independent readings of the same pages by the same model come out near 0.5 with an interval of about ±0.08 at 48 pages.
- **Cost:**
  - recording: $1.11 across four variants
  - judging so far: $8.9, of which Gemini $5.05, Qwen $2.6, MiMo $0.7 (each unit judged in both orders)

**What changed because of it:**
1. **Fixture fingerprints leave the levers out** (`7a6a69b`). A variant now replays the baseline's answers for every request it doesn't change, so a comparison differs only where the lever acts.
2. **Judging is chunked, round-robin over variants and judges, with early stopping** (`439a394`). A capped round no longer leaves later variants unjudged.
3. **Gemini is dropped** from round-level judging (the owner's decision); it cost about twice Qwen and seven times MiMo.

**Next: re-run round 1 as r01b** with the same four variants and the fixed fingerprints, judged by Qwen and MiMo, with a $10 cap.

**Standing lesson: measure the noise floor.** Re-asking an unchanged question yields a different answer often enough to matter (sign flips, misread dimensions). That is itself a quality problem, and a lever: voting or agreement over repeated readings, the mixed mode in the scheduling plan. It also means single comparisons of individual answers are unreliable; only aggregates over many units mean anything.

## r01b: the re-run with fixed fingerprints ($3.40)

Variants now replay the baseline's answers for requests they don't change, so comparisons differ only where a lever acts. Recording cost cents; judging (Qwen and MiMo, both orders, in chunks of 8 with early stopping) cost $3.34.

| Variant | Requests re-asked | Units changed | Judged | Win rate (90% interval) | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **text-layer** | 773 ($0.20) | 51 of 137 | 16, stopped early | **0.72** (0.56–0.88) | accepted |
| **lead-in** | 222 ($0.04) | 15 of 137 | 15, all | **0.71** (0.56–0.85) | accepted |
| **neighbours** | 187 ($0.06) | 43 of 137 | 24, stopped early | **0.68** (0.56–0.80) | accepted |
| table-filter | 53 (retries of recorded failures) | 12 of 56 pages | 12, all | 0.41 (0.23–0.57) | inconclusive |

**Against the predictions:**
- **text-layer:** predicted a visual win, largest on drawing sheets; confirmed on visual units.
- **lead-in:** predicted a small win; the win is larger than predicted, but on only 15 table units.
- **neighbours:** predicted a modest win; confirmed.
- **table-filter:** predicted a win on sheets; not confirmed. It changed only 12 pages, too few to decide.

**Why the judges preferred the variants** (their notes):
- **neighbours:** captured a pull-force value and a table the baseline missed, and avoided the baseline's duplicate claims.
- **text-layer:** fixed misread units (N·m² vs N·m³) and properties (shear modulus vs yield strength), and got "HARVEST HOME" right.
- **lead-in:** bound values to "Module A" and "Module B" instead of a generic "PV detail", and named "NREL 5-MW baseline wind turbine" as the entity.

**Warnings:**
- **One text-layer loss** was a title-block tile that yielded no claims at all. Worth checking that the transcript doesn't suppress extraction.
- **Early stopping at 16 units is aggressive.** Stopping as soon as an interval clears 0.5 inflates false positives, so the combination and a held-out check come next.
