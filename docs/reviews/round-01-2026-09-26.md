## Predictions (written before the results)

| Variant | Settings | Prediction | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **table-filter** | `table_filter: true`, compared page by page | Wins on drawing sheets and chart pages (it removes nonsense rows from false tables); neutral on reports; fewer requests | Losing a real fact found only by a false "table" row |
| **neighbours** | 400 characters before and after each text chunk | Modest win on text units: fewer vague entities ("scoring"), better conditions | Claims taken from the context, or duplicated from neighbours |
| **text-layer** | 1,500 characters of a region's text layer with each image | Win on visual units, largest on drawing sheets (small labels spelled right) | Longer prompts; the model copying text-layer fragments as claims |
| **lead-in** | 400 characters of text above a table with each row | Small win on table units (rows gain their table's subject) | Few real tables in the development set, so a weak signal |

A variant is accepted when its win rate's 90% interval lies above 0.5 and no kind of input loses clearly (interval entirely below 0.45).
