### reconcile: the representative change backfired

| Problem tag (all judges, both orders) | Baseline | Variant |
|---|---|---|
| duplicates | 90 | 78 |
| invented | 11 | **31** |
| conditions | 13 | 18 |

- **Merging worked:** fewer duplicates.
- **The chosen reading didn't.** Choosing the most informative reading (most condition words) chose readings from wide regions, which attach context from elsewhere on the page. On dc S-5xx, members were tagged "Structure South Elevation - Main", a label not on that page, and an NREL rated speed got "Region 3". The judges called them invented.
- **Reverted** (`0c2a2f0`): the most local reading represents; other wordings stay as occurrences.
- **Lesson for confidence scoring later:** a condition stated only by a wide-region reading is suspect.
- reconcile is re-tested in round 8 with rubric v3.

## What the judges' remarks say (both rounds' variants share these)

The remarks field (rubric v2) collected 187 remarks. They name failures both sides share, which a pairwise verdict can't show:

1. **Duplicates everywhere:** "raw extraction output without deduplication". This is what reconcile addresses.
2. **Invented chart readings at unlabelled points:** "inventing specific angles (23, 25) that are not labeled", "multiple conflicting values for the same graph points". The prompt already says plotted readings must be approximate, but the model still reads unmarked positions. *Candidate lever:* read charts only at labelled ticks or marked points, and state trends otherwise.
3. **Conditions lost in quotes:** "the page reads '…during the cool, martensite phase'; the condition is lost". *Candidate lever:* require the conditions to be taken from the quoted sentence.
4. **Definitions and equations missed:** "both sets miss the definitions of the discrete-time matrices". *Candidate lever:* a rule to extract defined symbols and equations as claims.
5. **Swapped identities in stacked or legend-keyed charts:** foam core and carbon skin swapped. Related to 2.
6. **A judge-side gap (fixed):** a section title the extractor rightly used was called invented, because judges saw only the page. Rubric v3 (`89bd4ff`) shows judges the page's headings.
