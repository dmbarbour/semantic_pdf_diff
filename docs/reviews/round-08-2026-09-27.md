### reconcile: the representative fix held; a false merge found

| Tag | Baseline | Variant |
|---|---|---|
| duplicates | 87 | **63** |
| invented | 10 | 7 |
| conditions | 16 | 11 |
| missing | 51 | **65** |

- **Why "missing" rose, from the analysis:** on dc S-301, "MODULE A – PV structural member" and "MODULE B – …" merged. "a" was a stopword, so "Module A" reduced to words contained in "Module B", and Module B's facts disappeared under Module A's wording.
- **Fixed** (`e7ea841`): single letters count as identifiers. The overall merge rate barely moved (13.6% → 13.5%), but these merges were exactly the drawing-sheet facts judges marked missing.
- **Where it stands by document:** text-heavy documents gain most (dc-manual 0.92, NREL 0.67). Drawing sheets were weakest (0.17–0.53), where the Module A/B merge hit.
- **Into the champion** (`benchmarks/champion.json`: `reconcile: true`). The held-out check comes with the next combination round.

### charts: the rule changed the wrong thing

- **The target failure survived:** on NREL p12 both sides still read gains at 23° and 25° on an axis that ends at 20°.
- **The trade:** on IEA the rule replaced specific readings with axis ranges and trends, some wrong ("increasing" where the curves peak and fall from stall). The judges preferred the specific readings (0.45 on IEA).
- **Tags moved little:** invented 31 → 26, misread 64 → 61.
- **Lesson:** prohibiting readings in the prompt trades precision for vagueness without stopping the out-of-range ones.
- **Next idea: a mechanical check instead.** A reading outside the chart's labelled tick range (from the text layer, or the axis labels the model itself reports) is flagged or lowered in confidence. Near-free, and it targets exactly the "23° on a 0–20° axis" failure.
