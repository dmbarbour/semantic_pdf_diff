## Results: all three accepted ($2.51)

Recording cost $0.23; judging $2.28 (Qwen $1.93, MiMo $0.35).

| Variant | Units changed | Judged | Win rate (90% interval) | By kind | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **stem** | 44 of 137 | 24, stopped early | **0.63** (0.51–0.75) | text 0.69 (0.52–0.84), table 0.51 (0.34–0.67) | accepted |
| **bands** | 36 of 137 | 16, stopped early | **0.83** (0.70–0.94) | visual only | accepted |
| **grow** | 48 of 137 | 16, stopped early | **0.80** (0.70–0.89) | visual only | accepted |

**By document** (units, mean score; too few to read much into):
- **stem:** dc-manual 0.80 (5), NREL 0.69 (7), calg 0.67 (3); IEA-15 0.31 (4) is the one loser
- **bands:** dc-manual 1.00 (5), NREL 0.88 (6), IEA-15 0.62 (5)
- **grow:** drawing sheets 1.00 (calg 2, dc 2), LCIT 1.00 (3), IEA-15 0.75, NREL 0.67, dc-manual 0.62, ken-cd 0.50

**Against the predictions:**
- **stem:** predicted a win on structured documents and neutral on reports; it won on text and was neutral on tables. The losses were on IEA-15, where the judges said the variant invented compound attributes.
- **bands:** predicted a visual win on reports; confirmed, larger than expected. The judges cited fewer invented chart readings (NREL gain plots) and fewer repeated values. One loss: a band read only part of an IEA page.
- **grow:** predicted a modest win on sheets; it won on sheets and on report tiles too. On calg the baseline misread a cut "2x4 cedar handrail" note.

**Why the judges preferred the variants** (their notes):
- **bands:** readings consistent with the chart's axes, where the baseline's contradicted them; no ten-fold near-duplicates.
- **grow:** values bound to named parts ("Module A/B segment lengths") rather than bare dimension strings.
- **stem:** fewer duplicated claims under two entity names ("blade-pitch controller" and "baseline blade-pitch controller").

**Warnings:**
- **Early stopping at 16–24 units** again; the combination and held-out checks come next, as in round 2.
- **bands and grow overlap on report pages.** With both on, report pages get bands and wide pages (drawing sheets) get grown grid tiles, so the combination tests each where it acts.
- **MiMo times out on long units.** At 600 s with two retries a chunk stalled for 30 minutes; at 300 s with none, MiMo lost 14 of 112 verdicts (Qwen 2), mostly long tables and full-page visuals. Those units were judged by Qwen alone. Timeouts and retries are now round settings (`judge_timeout`, `judge_retries`).

**A recurring complaint: duplicated claims.** Across all three variants the judges penalized repeated values and the same claim under two entity names. That is a candidate lever for round 5: a rule against repeats, or merging near-duplicates before judging.
