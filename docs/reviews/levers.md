# Lever index

Every query lever tried in the [improvement rounds](../plans/query-improvement-2026-09-26.md): what it does, what we expected, what happened, why we think it happened, and what to try next.

- **Detail:** each round's review in this folder.
- **Per-unit clues:** `benchmarks/rounds/<round>/pairs-<variant>/analysis.json`, browsable as `insights.html` in the round folder (regenerate with `scripts/run_round.py <round> --only report`).
- **Win rates:** the variant's share of pairwise preferences against the baseline, judged in both orders, with a 90% interval (a bootstrap until 2026-09-28; since then cluster-robust, by page region).
- **This index is the lever catalogue** (the owner, 2026-09-28): every lever idea gets a row when it's raised, built or not. Levers not accepted or parked are tried again against later champions, and other ways of doing one idea are tried as its variants.

### Table structure (`table_structure`) and the PDF table heuristics
- **Mechanism:** a PDF table's lines go through heuristics (`tables.py`: rows joined across row boundaries without a drawn rule in a mostly ruled table; a two-line header merged; a stacked table, styled as the header, split off), then, for a part with a suspect line, the structure query (`tablestructure.py`): its lines tagged with signals, its image, rules from a closed vocabulary applied mechanically, examples copied from the image checked, the outcome reviewed. ### Quote matching (`quote_match`)
- **Found in the overall review:** the verbatim quote check dropped a third of all text claims (2,826 of 8,661 in recorded answers). They were mostly elided quotes, line-end hyphenation, Unicode variants, and table cells joined with "|".
- **`fragments`:**
  - normalizes Unicode, case, dashes, quote marks and line-end hyphens
  - accepts quotes whose "…" or "|" parts each appear, contiguous and in order
  - recovers about 1,070 claims
  - **Accepted in r09b:** 0.66, text 0.77. Missing fell sharply; misbound rose.
- **Post-mortem (2026-09-30, Gemini reading r09b's samples):** "…" may join distant fragments (separate axis labels, far-apart table cells), so it can verify a misbound claim. That fits misbound rising.
  - **Next:** cap how far "…" may reach (no crossing a blank line or another block), or accept "|" joins only in tables and "…" only in prose.
  - The weak visual partition in that batch (0.44) was mostly merged-claim wording shown in visual units (a bug since fixed), which the analyst couldn't know.
- **`excerpts`:** also accepts words read in order across a pseudo-table. It recovers about 1,700 claims, but let chart-axis labels through as values (r09: 0.51). Not accepted.
- **Measurement notes:**
  - No requests change, so testing costs judging only.
  - An A/A control in the same round (r09: 0.48 ± 0.12) gives the noise floor.

  - **Got:** snapped halves the lines cut and read twice but keeps nearly every tile. XY-cut cuts 80% to 90% fewer lines on wide pages; the knob slide's misbound claims are gone, and slides go from six tiles to two (h2-amr p8). On the two gallery drawing sheets, XY-cut's larger tiles found 20 and 26 fewer distinct values than the grid's overlapping tiles. One reading each and no key, so not every lost value was right.
  - **Next (tweak):** on drawing sheets, XY-cut blocks no larger than a tile; a block with no gap cut into strips, not the grid.
- **Render by area** (`render_area`): a region rendered to the pixel area of a square at `image_side`, so a long band isn't enlarged by the model. A variant of the idea below (*Render every image at gemma-4's budget size*).
  - **Got:** no gain on Letter pages (ten corpus documents: facts right 372 → 366, loose 57 → 48, inexact 62 → 69: likely noise). It's what makes long tiles affordable for XY-cut and wide bands.

### Tile choice (`tile_choice`)

Built in [tile selection](../plans/tile-selection-2026-10-10.md) milestone 5; `measures` a default since milestone 8 (2026-10-12). A drawing sheet keeps the tiler's cut: XY-cut's larger tiles lost facts there. Each tiler's cut is measured on the page (`tilechoice.choose`), and the page gets the cut at least as good as the rest on every measure. Where none is, `measures` keeps today's cut unless another beats it, and `model` asks the model among the cuts no other beats, with the calibrated question (`tilechoice.py`, milestone 4).

- **The candidates:** bands where they apply today, the grid, snapped and XY-cut, each as tiles_for makes it.
- **The measures** (each fewer is better):
  - lines cut
  - figures split
  - lines and graphics missed
  - tiles of decoration or blank alone
  - lines read twice
  - lines too small to read: capitals under 7 px at the tile's detail, gemma-4's eye-test threshold
  - images sent, after the keep rules
- **Got, by the measures alone:**
  - The controlled corpus: 450 → 408 tiles; facts right 3,438 → 3,439; misbound 261 → 236; loose 490 → 426. The knobs' misbound claims are gone (17 → 0), and the plan sheets keep their facts.
  - The gallery's drawing sheet scical p7 loses 20 values and gains 12 against today's grid.
  - The rule's own faults, found in earlier runs and fixed before these:
    - Ties were asked.
    - Bands were compared on tiles before their text-only bands were skipped.
    - A cut beating today's could be passed over for one that didn't.
    - With no measure of detail, XY-cut's larger tiles won on drawing sheets.
- **Got, asked:**
  - The model picks the cut that looks cleanest, mostly XY-cut, which has the fewest tiles.
  - On the corpus's six plan sheets it chose XY-cut each time: facts 231 → 207 (the rule alone: 231). On the gallery's scical sheet it lost 27 values, and 10 of the h2-amr slide's 18.
  - The question asks about cuts and coverage, not how small the text becomes, and calibration tested only that.
- **Ties** (2026-10-12): asked, the model may name tilings it can't choose between, and the page is read with both. Calibration's tie arms pass (known-good alone 94.3%, ties 0.7%; near cases 91.8%, ties 4.6%). Every tie held the known-good, and the both-orders ties found a fault in the measures (a figure's white margin counted as split). No corpus page tied.
- **Since:** `measures` became a default in milestone 8, after the held-out pages and the slices were measured. `model` stays off; offering it only cuts no worse on small text is an idea below.

### Region labels (`region_labels`)

Built in [tile selection](../plans/tile-selection-2026-10-10.md) milestone 6, off by default. The model labels the tiles a page sends (`regionlabels.py`), once a page (`Platform.sent_tiles`): text, table, chart, drawing, photo or scan, decoration, blank, mixed, nonsense or other.

- **The rules:**
  - Tiles of text, decoration or blank aren't sent, but only where the measures find no graphic that counts in them.
  - On a drawing sheet, text is sent.
  - A nonsense tile is joined to its neighbour.
- **Calibrated** (`scripts/labelling.py`, gemma-4):
  - Labels right 74.5%.
  - Content lost 0.8% (called text, decoration or blank) and tiles saved 97.3%, the pass mark.
  - Nonsense is almost never seen (3.9%).
- **Got** (the controlled corpus):
  - 450 → 390 tiles with no fact lost.
  - With tile_choice's rule, 359 tiles and 3 facts more than today.
  - As first built, the model called a plan sheet's small print blank (17 facts lost). The measures' veto and sending a sheet's text fixed it.
- **Since:** in milestone 8, with the new defaults, the labels saved 25 tiles for 125 questions, facts and loose and misbound claims as without them; skipping by the measures alone (`skip_text_only: except_sheets`) saved 34 and cost 4 facts. Neither is a default. One question for the cut and the labels, and labels on scans, are ideas below.

### Refinement (`another_tiling`, `halve_parts`, `refine_figures`, `least_half`)

Built in [tile selection](../plans/tile-selection-2026-10-10.md) milestone 7, all off by default (`least_half` at 25%, as before). A partial tile's next reads come from `Platform.refined_tile`, given its answer.

- **The levers:**
  - `another_tiling` (`retiling.py`): the page's next candidate cut by the measures, its tiles holding what the partial tile cuts, each read once a page. Always (`measures`), where the answer says it's cut (`said`), or where it names the edges cutting content when a rule asks (`asked`). Halves where the measures find nothing cut.
  - `halve_parts`: halves cut around the detected figures the tile holds, where they can be.
  - `refine_figures: again`: a tile mostly one figure looked at again whole, told its claims and issues.
  - `least_half`: the least share of what a tile holds each half keeps.
- **Calibrated** (`scripts/asking.py`, gemma-4): asked to name a cut, it names one for 48.8% of tiles that cut content and 0.8% of whole ones; today's prompt, 31.2% and 10.5%. Neither passes (half the cut tiles).
- **Got** (every tile refined, `scripts/refinement.py`): `measures` with `halve_parts` is the best arm. On the controlled corpus, refined reads 197 → 135, their facts right 453 → 514, loose 93 → 23, misbound 38 → 24; whole documents' facts unchanged. On 16 real pages, refined reads 257 → 151, quotes not in the page 78 → 54, claims bound to other words 246 → 114.
- **Defaults since milestone 8** (2026-10-12): `another_tiling: measures` and `halve_parts`. A tile continued at the claim limit is halved, not retiled: read under the other cut, HabEx p403's dense table lost 43 of 454 values. Refinement is rare in a normal run, so the forced measure is where they show.

## Measurement notes (affect how to read the table)

- **Paired documents since 2026-10-14:** a lever is judged on the controlled corpus by `scripts/paired.py` ([judging a lever](../samples-and-testing.md#judging-a-lever)): arms share answers, each document's change is summed, with a sign-flip p-value, against null arms where a lever rewords many queries. Three do-nothing image rules compared pairwise called 7 of 83 measures a change at p < 0.1; facts right never moved by more than 6 of 3,409, while claim counts swung by up to 221.
- **A/A noise:** two readings of the same pages score about 0.5 ± 0.08 over 48 units (r01), so single units mean little.
- **Early stopping at 16–24 units inflates wins;** the combination rounds (r02, r04) at 40–48 units are more honest.
- **Before round 7,** units with more than 25 claims showed judges separately drawn samples, which added noise to visual units in every round. This was fixed in `5850dfd`.
- **Rubric v2 from round 7:** document administration is neutral; judges tag each side's problems and may leave remarks.
- **Free cost saving noticed in r05d's analysis:** the model kept reporting blank tiles. Built as `skip_empty` (see above).
