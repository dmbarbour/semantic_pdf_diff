## Owner's decisions (2026-09-27)

- **Readings of one fact are one claim.** Different queries over the same content are one logical read: "the LLM equivalent of squinting at it or looking from a few slightly different angles". Built as `reconcile` (`b5643b7`):
  - applied when evidence is read, so no request changes
  - every reading kept as an occurrence, with its own wording
- **A small overview showing where each tile comes from** (the owner's suggestion). Built as `tile_locator` (`e198bf7`): a 384 px page thumbnail with the tile outlined in red, as a second image.

## Predictions (written before the results)

| Variant | Prediction | Main risk |
|---|---|---|
| **reconcile** | Clear win wherever it merges. 13.6% fewer claims on the champion's development runs (4,388 → 3,792, 458 merged groups), and duplication was the judges' most common complaint. | A wrong merge hides a distinct fact behind another's wording. In a sample of 25 merges, none was wrong after tightening the rules (entity and attribute words must be contained, not just overlap). |
| **locator** | Modest win on tile units: bindings to titles and labels outside the crop, and fewer "unclear what this is" readings | The model reads the thumbnail's tiny text and extracts from outside the tile; or it ignores the thumbnail |

**Measurement note:** reconcile changes how claims are counted. The judges see merged claims as single claims, which is the point, but a judge could prefer the unmerged side for "coverage" if a merge looks like an omission.

The analysis files (`pairs-*/analysis.json`, `insights.html`), the first built for a live round, explain most of it.

**A measurement flaw (fixed in `5850dfd`):**
- Units with more than 25 claims showed judges separately drawn samples of each side.
- **reconcile:** on IEA p2 (37 and 36 claims, 34 shared) the judges compared different subsets.
- **locator:** its losing drawing units had 100–300 claims; on calg p62–66 p5, 23 of the 25 claims shown per side differed.
- Those verdicts measured the sample, not the lever. The flaw affected visual units in every earlier round too; see the [lever index](levers.md).

**reconcile, a lever flaw (fixed in the same commit):**
- On NREL p3 the merged claim took the best-ranked region's wording, which lacked "isolated tower (without rotor-nacelle mass)", a condition another reading had. The judges marked the condition lost.
- The most informative reading now represents a merge.
- **The text win (0.72) is the lever working:** fewer duplicates, and the judges said so.

**locator, split by document type:**
- **Wins on reports:** IEA 0.82 (8 units), LCIT 1.00 (3).
- **Losses on drawing sheets and the manual:** dc p31–38 0.17, calg 0.25–0.38, ken 0.00, dc-manual 0.00.
- The model's issue notes never mention the thumbnail, and nothing suggests it extracted from it.
- **Hypothesis:** on reports the tiles are full-width bands, and the thumbnail says which figure or section a band belongs to. On dense sheets a 384 px thumbnail is illegible and adds nothing (or noise).
- The drawing losses are also the units most affected by the sampling flaw, so the split needs a re-judge before it's believed.
