# Round 6: merged readings and tile locators

- **Date:** 2026-09-27
- **Plan:** [query improvement in rounds](../plans/query-improvement-2026-09-26.md)
- **Research:** [duplicate claims](../research/duplicate-claims-2026-09-27.md)
- **Baseline:** the round-4 champion
- **Judges:** Qwen3.5-397B and MiMo-V2.6-Pro, both orders, chunks of 8, early stopping from 16 units; 32 units at most; 450 s timeout, no retries; $10 cap

## Owner's decisions (2026-09-27)

- **Readings of one fact are one claim.** Different queries over the same content are one logical read: "the LLM equivalent of squinting at it or looking from a few slightly different angles". Built as `reconcile` (`b5643b7`):
  - applied when evidence is read, so no request changes
  - every reading kept as an occurrence, with its own wording
- **A small overview showing where each tile comes from** (the owner's suggestion). Built as `tile_locator` (`e198bf7`): a 384 px page thumbnail with the tile outlined in red, as a second image.

## Predictions (written before the results)

| Variant | Prediction | Main risk |
|---|---|---|
| **reconcile** | Clear win wherever it merges. 13.6% fewer claims on the champion's development runs (4,388 → 3,792, 458 merged groups), and duplication was the judges' most common complaint. | A wrong merge hides a distinct fact behind another's wording. In a sample of 25 merges, none was wrong after tightening the rules (entity and attribute words must be contained, not just overlap). |
| **locator** | Modest win on tile units: bindings to titles and labels outside the crop, and fewer "unclear what this is" readings | The model reads the thumbnail's tiny text and extracts from outside the tile; or it ignores the thumbnail |

**Measurement note:** reconcile changes how claims are counted. The judges see merged claims as single claims, which is the point, but a judge could prefer the unmerged side for "coverage" if a merge looks like an omission.
