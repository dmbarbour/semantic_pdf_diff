- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Plan:** [query improvement in rounds](../plans/query-improvement-2026-09-26.md)
- **Method:** the revised one, after the [overall review](overall-review-2026-09-28.md):
  - a fixed sample of 32 units per variant, decided once
  - MiMo-V2.6-Pro on every unit, with Qwen3.5-397B on units MiMo leaves unsettled
  - rubric v4: set samples declared, and the extractor's context shown

  - failed verdicts retried once
- **Baseline:** the champion, now with the bug fixes: rotated sheets read in displayed order, the table lead-in from text displayed above the table, the generic-reading merge fix.

## Why excerpt quotes

- A text claim is dropped when its quote isn't verbatim in its chunk.
- Across every recorded text answer (8,661 claims), **2,826 were dropped (33%)**. Most of them were:
  - elided quotes ("structural-damping ratio ... of 5% critical")
  - line-end hyphenation ("linear-\nspring")
  - Unicode variants
  - table cells joined with "|"
  - a header read in order with its value
- `quote_match: excerpts` keeps quotes whose parts appear in order in the chunk. That recovers **1,629 (58%)**, about 19% of all text claims.
- Quotes taken from the context (95) and paraphrases (about 1,100) stay rejected.
- It changes no requests, only which recorded claims are kept, so the round costs judging only.
- It speaks to the judges' most common remark: "both sets miss" a fact, 159 of 375 remarks.
