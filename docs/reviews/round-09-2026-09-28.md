# Round 9: excerpt quotes, and an A/A control

- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Plan:** [query improvement in rounds](../plans/query-improvement-2026-09-26.md)
- **Method:** the revised one, after the [overall review](overall-review-2026-09-28.md):
  - a fixed sample of 32 units per variant, decided once
  - MiMo-V2.6-Pro on every unit, with Qwen3.5-397B on units MiMo leaves unsettled
  - rubric v4: set samples declared, and the extractor's context shown
  - units over 25 claims cut into page bands
  - failed verdicts retried once
- **Baseline:** the champion, now with the bug fixes: rotated sheets read in displayed order, the table lead-in from text displayed above the table, the generic-reading merge fix.

## Why excerpt quotes

- A text claim is dropped when its quote isn't verbatim in its chunk.
- Across every recorded text answer (8,661 claims), **2,826 were dropped (33%)**. Most of them were:
  - elided quotes ("structural-damping ratio ... of 5% critical")
  - line-end hyphenation ("linear-\nspring")
  - Unicode variants
  - table cells joined with "|"
  - a header read in order with its value
- `quote_match: excerpts` keeps quotes whose parts appear in order in the chunk. That recovers **1,629 (58%)**, about 19% of all text claims.
- Quotes taken from the context (95) and paraphrases (about 1,100) stay rejected.
- It changes no requests, only which recorded claims are kept, so the round costs judging only.
- It speaks to the judges' most common remark: "both sets miss" a fact, 159 of 375 remarks.

## Predictions (written before the results)

| Variant | Prediction | Main risk |
|---|---|---|
| **excerpts** | A win on text units (0.6–0.7) and a small one on tables; "missing" tags fall | Recovered claims read across a pseudo-table could be misbound ("Science Band IR … 69,000"). Tags will show whether "misbound" rises. |
| **aa** (the champion's image tasks asked afresh) | About 0.5, with the interval covering 0.5: the noise floor of re-asking under the new method | If it lands far from 0.5, fresh readings differ systematically from recorded ones (e.g. recorded retries), and levers that re-ask everything would need correcting |

### The A/A control: the noise floor, as predicted

- **Re-asking the champion's image tasks gives 0.48, with the interval spanning 0.5.** Under the new method, fresh readings don't differ systematically from recorded ones.
- **The interval's half-width at 32 units is about ±0.12.** A lever therefore needs a true effect of roughly 0.62 or more to be accepted reliably at this sample size.
- **Its tags barely move,** except "quote", which is noisy (9 → 21).
- **Merged readings reach across kinds.** Re-asking only image regions also changed 8 text and table units, because fresh image readings merge differently with text and table claims (a table unit gained a separately listed "steel rod 3 1/2""). The control measures re-asking noise on the output as it's read, merges included.

### The method in practice

- **MiMo timed out on 22 of 128 verdicts at 450 s,** even after one retry. Escalation sent 19 units to Qwen, and Qwen cost twice MiMo ($1.22 against $0.61).
- Failures no longer hold chunks, so future rounds give MiMo 900 s: wall time, not money.

  - HabEx 0.73 (12)

  - ken-manual 0.00 (1)
- **Fragments stays in the champion.** The development result was a clear win, and the held-out one agrees in direction and size. Its interval touches 0.5 only because 21 units is all the held-out set has.
- **A consistent visual weakness:** 0.44 on development (9 units) and 0.40 held-out (10). Neither blocks, but the direction repeats.
  - **Suspected cause:** the merge. Recovered text claims now absorb image readings of the same fact, so image units show the text reading's wording and its elided quote.
  - **Next:** check in the analysis whether the losing visual units' variant-only claims are merged text readings.
