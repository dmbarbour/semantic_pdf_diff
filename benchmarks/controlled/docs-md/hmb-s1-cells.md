# Harbour Monitoring Buoy: System Requirements

## 1 Scope

This document states the requirements for the harbour monitoring buoy. Each requirement is identified, stated and verified as below.

## 2 Requirements

**Table 1. Buoy requirements**

| ID | Name | Text | Rationale | Verification Method | Requirement Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HMB-REQ-101 | Survival Temperature | The buoy hull shall withstand ambient temperatures from -42 °C to +58 °C without damage. | The buoy winters in an unheated yard and is deployed through the summer. | Test |  |
| HMB-REQ-102 | Data Retention | In the event of a loss of main power, the data logger shall retain every logged record for at least 85 hours. | Field crews may take several days to reach a buoy after an outage. | Test, Analysis |  |
| HMB-REQ-103 | Hull Colour | The buoy hull above the waterline shall be painted international orange. | Mariners must see the buoy by day against open water. | Inspection |  |
| HMB-REQ-104 | Record Format | The data logger should store its records in a documented, non-proprietary file format. | Records outlive the software that wrote them. | Inspection |  |
| HMB-REQ-105 | Remote Update | The logger firmware may be updated over the satellite link. | Visits to the buoy cost a boat day each. | Demonstration |  |
| HMB-REQ-106 | Mooring Inspection | The operator will inspect the mooring chain every 11 months. | Chain wear is the leading cause of lost buoys. | Inspection |  |
| HMB-REQ-107 | Transmit Interval | While the buoy is moored, the telemetry unit shall report its position and water temperature at intervals of no more than 30 minutes. | The harbour office plots positions in near real time. | Demonstration |  |
| HMB-REQ-108 | Lamp Visibility | The navigation lamp shall be visible at a range of at least 4.4 nautical miles on a clear night. | The buoy marks a channel used at night. | Analysis |  |
