# Harbour Monitoring Buoy: Risk Register

## 1 Purpose

This register lists the risks to the harbour monitoring buoy's service and how each is treated.

## 2 Risks

**Table 1. Risk register**

| Risk ID | Risk | Likelihood | Consequence | Mitigation | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | If the mooring chain wears through, the buoy may drift into the shipping channel. | Medium | A hazard to navigation until the buoy is recovered. | Replace any chain link worn by more than 20 percent of its diameter. | Operator |
| R-02 | If the satellite service is withdrawn, positions stop reaching the harbour office. | Low | The buoy's position is unknown between visits. | Keep a second telemetry provider under contract. | Harbour office |
| R-03 | If seabirds foul the solar panel, the battery may not recharge in winter. | High | Data gaps through the darkest months. | Fit bird spikes around the panel frame. | Operator |
| R-04 | If the lamp fails at night, the channel is unmarked. | Low | Vessels may stray from the channel. | Broadcast a notice to mariners within 3 hours of a reported failure. | Harbour office |
