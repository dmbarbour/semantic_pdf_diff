# Ridgeback roller coaster

## 1 Ride Overview

Ridgeback is a steel roller coaster with 6,694 ft of track and 5 inversions. A ride lasts 121 s and reaches a maximum speed of 68.6 mph.

In revision B the lift hill was raised from 178 ft to 230 ft. The first drop falls 237 ft at an angle of 72.4°, and the maximum vertical acceleration is 3.8 g.

## 2 Trains

The ride operates 3 trains of 6 cars, each car seating 4 riders, for an hourly capacity of 1,124 riders/h. An empty train weighs 12,491 lb.

## 4 Structure

The track is carried on 312 support columns using 971 tons of steel. The structure shall withstand a design wind speed of 98 mph (3-second gust).
