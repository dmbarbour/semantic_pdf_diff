# Cyclone roller coaster

## 1 Ride Overview

Cyclone is a steel roller coaster with 6,112 ft of track and 3 inversions. A ride lasts 152 s and reaches a maximum speed of 74.5 mph.

The chain lift rises 195 ft. The main drop falls 208 ft at an angle of 71.6°.

## 2 Trains

The ride operates 2 trains of 6 cars, each car seating 4 riders, for an hourly capacity of 1,174 riders/h. An empty train weighs 12,375 lb. Each train is 63 ft long.

## 3 Track Elements

**Table 1. Track elements**

| Element | Height (ft) | Entry speed (mph) | Peak g |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main drop | 208 | 54.4 | 2.8 |
| Vertical loop | 68 | 52.6 | 3.4 |
| Heartline roll | 76 | 47.2 | 2.1 |
| Airtime hill | 80 | 62.7 | 2.2 |
| Bunny hop | 69 | 55.9 | 2.4 |

## 4 Structure

The track is carried on 276 steel bents using 951 tons of steel. The structure shall withstand a design wind speed of 110 mph (3-second gust).
