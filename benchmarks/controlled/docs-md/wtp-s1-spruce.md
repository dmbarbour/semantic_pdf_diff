# Spruce Lake Water Treatment Plant

## 1 Basis of Design

The Spruce Lake Water Treatment Plant is sized for a design flow of 18.2 MGD (average day) and a peak flow of 38.4 MGD on the maximum day, serving a population of 69,135 persons.

Raw water turbidity reaches 24.7 NTU (95th percentile), measured over the last five years. Finished water turbidity shall not exceed 0.12 NTU in 95% of monthly samples.

## 3 Chemical Systems

Alum is dosed at 17.4 mg/L on the average day and up to 63.3 mg/L on the maximum day. Chlorine is fed at 3.3 mg/L ahead of the clearwell.

## 4 Process Units

The rapid mix basin provides a detention time of 38 s at design flow, with a velocity gradient of 890 1/s. Flocculation follows in 3 stages, for a total detention time of 20.5 min at design flow.

There are 4 sedimentation basins, each 164 ft long and 46 ft wide, with a side water depth of 12.4 ft; the surface loading rate is 0.53 gpm/ft² at design flow.

Granular filtration uses 10 granular media beds of 599 ft² each. With one bed out of service, the filtration rate is 2.3 gpm/ft².
